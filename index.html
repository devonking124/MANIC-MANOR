HOW TO PLAY

  • HOLD GRIP + PULL the air toward you to fly. Let go mid-pull to fling and glide. Two hands = hand-over-hand.
  • LEFT STICK slow glide · RIGHT STICK ←→ snap turn, ↑↓ float up/down.
  • Your SOUL (the red heart) floats at your chest. Only the heart can be hit. Duck, lean and fly.
  • POKE the big menu buttons with your hand, or POINT + TRIGGER.
  • FIGHT: swing a controller through the glowing target when the sweeping line hits the center. On the beat = CRITICAL.
  • WHITE = dodge · BLUE = hold still · ORANGE = keep moving · GREEN = heals · YELLOW guests loop around.
  • GRAZE bullets (pass close without touching) to build TP. TP powers special ACTs.
  • YELLOW SOUL: hold TRIGGER (desktop: hold click) to shoot stars where your hand points. CYAN SOUL: your heart rides the cables — pull (or WASD/Q/E) along a cable, pull toward another cable at a junction to switch.
  • GLITCH bullets flip BLUE/ORANGE on every beat. Pop-up windows close when you POKE their [X].
  • TYPE ATTACK (boss 2's FIGHT): punch each glowing key on its beat (desktop: press that letter).
  • Pause: Y / B button (VR) or Esc (desktop).
  • Desktop: WASD + Q/E move · mouse look · click slash/select · Space pull-dash · 1-9 menu · arrows dance.
  • Debug: H hitboxes · G god mode · N next phase · B beat grid · [ ] nudge music offset · P perf stats.
